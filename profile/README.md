## HomeAccounting

**Logging money takes seconds — or nothing at all. And it stays yours.**

Most finance apps die at the same point: logging a transaction is tedious, so
you stop. HomeAccounting attacks capture from three directions.

### Three ways to capture

- **Bank import** — monobank sync, PrivatBank / CSV / XLSX. Categorized on
  arrival; you just confirm.
- **Free-text** — "coffee 45, taxi 200, groceries 380" becomes real
  transactions, with your accounts and categories.
- **Telegram** — the same capture, from a chat you already have open.

### Same product — you choose who runs it

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ☁️ **[Free cloud](https://homeaccounting.com/app)** | Nothing to install. Free while in beta, encrypted at rest, updates itself. |
| 🖥️ **[Self-host](https://github.com/homeaccounting/docker)** | One Docker Compose command. Your data never leaves your machine. |
